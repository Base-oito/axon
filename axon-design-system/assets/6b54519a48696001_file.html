    In plain terms

    A firm, an engine, and a funnel.

    What is Alpha Deal?

    The agentic underwriting firm for subsidized and incentive-financed development. We pair autonomous AI agents with domain experts to run the whole pre-application funnel — site to submittable deal — in days, not weeks.

    What does it do?

    Takes a site through the full LIHTC funnel: screening, zoning, density-yield maximization, program design, credit-path selection, capital stack, go / no-go. Every unit and every dollar traced to a program, a rule, and a source.

    Who uses it?

    Affordable and mixed-income developers, housing investors and syndicators, CDFIs, and public agencies — the teams doing incentive-financed development, where the yield-and-financing maze kills most deals before they start.

    How is it priced?

    Free request, paid delivery. Requesting a deal costs nothing — you upload the site, we run the funnel. You pay when the deliverable is ready. Plans are an allowance of analyses per month — you buy outcomes, not seats, not tool access.
